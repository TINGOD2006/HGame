## 2. 最後一次編修的原始提示詞

下列英文為工具輸入原文，保留以利追溯；提示詞中的尺寸、材質意圖不等於生成結果完全符合。

~~~text
Edit this character reference sheet into a tasteful ADULT fashion variation. The subject is an unmistakably adult 24-year-old woman: retain clearly adult facial structure and adult anatomical proportions, recognizable honey-blonde ponytail with blue ribbon, teal eyes, and the simple hand-drawn Japanese RPG cel-shading technique. Make her adult build a little slimmer and leaner through the waist, arms and legs, healthy rather than underweight, with a natural small bust. Replace the travel jacket and buttoned shirt with an opaque ivory off-the-shoulder knit top with long fitted sleeves, showing shoulders but fully covering the bust, with no visible cleavage. Replace the full flared skirt with a high-waisted navy wrap midi skirt, modest side slit ending near the knee; add a slim dark belt with a small gold buckle. Simple brown ankle boots and a small pendant, without bulky backpack or travel pouches. This is ordinary grown-up fashion, not lingerie or a costume uniform. Keep full-body neutral relaxed standing pose and two face portraits; a confident playful adult smile, not a seductive expression. Pale plain background. Keep crisp dark outlines, simple flat colors and restrained hard-edged shadows; no glossy skin or painterly rendering. Clearly adult face and proportions are essential; no de-aging, adolescent or childlike face, chibi anatomy, school uniforms, sailor collars, student styling, underwear, nudity, fetish accessories or sexual pose. Non-explicit tasteful adult fashion character design. No text or watermark.
~~~

## 3. 上游參考圖的生成提示詞

上游圖使用四張使用者提供的其他遊戲圖片作畫法參考；它們沒有納入儲存庫。下列提示詞重點是借鑑畫法，排除校服、未成年外觀、原人物及原遊戲介面。

~~~text
Use case: stylized-concept. Make an entirely NEW original NONSEXUAL character illustration in the drawing technique of the supplied Japanese indie RPG sprite/portrait references. All four inputs are ONLY rendering/linework references, not identities, ages, costumes or body types. Especially do not reproduce the school uniforms or student characters in references 1 and 2, and do not copy exposed or exaggerated bodies in references 3 and 4. The new character is an unmistakably adult 24-year-old petite female adventurer with natural adult anatomy, adult facial structure, a small natural bust under modest opaque clothing, warm honey-blonde hair in a simple ponytail tied with a small blue ribbon, blue-green almond eyes, and an upbeat confident expression. Full-body neutral standing pose plus two small portrait expressions (friendly smile and amused smile) on a pale plain background. Clothing is an original simple ADULT fantasy adventurer costume: opaque ivory crewneck blouse, a short dusty-rose travel jacket ending at the waist, a navy A-line knee-length skirt, low-heeled brown ankle boots, compact belt pouch. No school or student elements. The main priority is a genuine classic hand-drawn Japanese 2D RPG character illustration: economical rounded dark linework, simplified facial planes, small understated nose and mouth, clear expressive anime eyes, chunky coherent hair locks, restrained local-color shadows and one broad flat hair highlight. Mostly flat color with one or two cel-shadow tones, very few details, little rendering. Keep adult proportions, no chibi or adolescent appearance. Nonsexual costume and pose. Avoid the glossy overrendered contemporary illustration look: no oily skin, no airbrush shine, no painted strand-by-strand hair, no realistic face rendering, no detailed fabric textures, no dramatic lighting. Avoid seductive expressions, sexual posing, cleavage, underwear, thigh-highs, school uniforms, sailor collars, cropped school blouses, fetish framing, text, logos and interface elements. Clean original adult game-character reference illustration.
~~~
